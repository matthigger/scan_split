Sort scanned exam pages into one PDF per question. Everything runs in your browser; PDFs are never uploaded.

Files are stacked in name order. Page layout, page count and headers are learned from the scans; nothing needs to be set up.

    Each card is the average of every sheet of that type (handwriting fades out, the printed page remains). Choose where each type goes; backs always travel with their front.

    Click to select (shift-click for a range), then re-route the selection. Double-click or ⤢ to inspect a sheet.
